Clerisy answers one question after every meeting: **what changed in the project?** It records your meetings without bots, transcribes them locally or in the cloud, runs AI analysis to compare the transcript against your living project state, and presents a set of evidence-backed proposals — each quoting the exact transcript line that supports it. You approve what makes sense, reject what doesn't, and Clerisy writes a new versioned snapshot of your project.

## How Clerisy works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build your project state">
    Create a project and add what you already know — requirements, decisions, tasks, risks, deadlines, and open questions. Each item is typed, owned, and versioned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record or import a meeting">
    Use `Ctrl+Shift+M` or the Capture button to start recording. Clerisy captures your microphone and system audio separately — no meeting bot, no invite required. You can also import `.vtt`, `.srt`, Zoom `.txt`, or audio/video files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Impact Report">
    After the meeting ends, Clerisy transcribes the audio and runs AI analysis against your project state. Each proposed change is backed by a verbatim quote from the transcript. Accept, edit, or reject each proposal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply changes">
    Click **Apply** to write a new versioned snapshot of your project state. Every item's history records which meeting changed it and what was said.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key capabilities

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Botless Capture" icon="microphone">
    Records mic and system audio as separate streams. Works with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Slack, Discord — anything that uses your microphone.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hallucination Guard" icon="shield-check">
    Every AI proposal must cite a verbatim transcript quote. Quotes are validated in code — proposals without real evidence are discarded before you see them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring Your Own Model" icon="brain">
    Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible server. Keys are encrypted with Windows DPAPI and never leave your machine.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Local Speech-to-Text" icon="server">
    Free offline English transcription via sherpa-onnx after a one-time 665 MB download. No upload, no API key, no per-minute cost.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ask the Project" icon="circle-question">
    Ask questions like "Why did the launch date move?" and get answers grounded exclusively in your project's own items and meeting transcripts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share Reports" icon="share-nodes">
    Export Impact Reports as Slack messages, WhatsApp text, Markdown files, or PDFs — with or without evidence quotes and the full transcript.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Clerisy v1 is a Windows-only desktop application. macOS support is planned for a future release.
